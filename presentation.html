Problem vs. Solution

Direct breakdown of conventional classroom friction and how ClassSync solves it.

The Classroom Problem
  • Students lose focus during one way slide lectures.
  • Students copy or search quiz questions on phones during live tests.
  • Students hesitate to raise hands in front of 50 classmates when stuck on a slide.
  • Looking down at phone screens breaks classroom attention.
Our Solution (ClassSync)
  • Eyes Up Quiz Controller: Questions and options appear only on the projector; student phones show only 4 colored buttons.
  • Interactive Live Slide Presenter: Host controls slides on the big screen while students send live floating comments and tap an anonymous Confused button.
  • Millisecond Speed Scoring: Fast correct answers earn higher points, driving live competition on the projector leaderboard.
  • Instant QR Join: 50 students join in seconds with custom avatars and zero complex sign up.

Anti Cheat Quiz Arena & Scoring Engine

Blind buzzer delivery coupled with millisecond speed decay scoring.

Anti Cheat Mechanics

  • Blind Phone Controller: Questions, answers, timers, and live scores are never transmitted to student phones, preventing cheating and screen sharing.
  • Automated 3 Stage Game Show Flow: After the question timer expires, the host screen automatically advances from Question to Option Matrix to Leaderboard without manual clicking.
  • Configurable Timing Engine: Host can tune Question Time, Matrix Reveal Time, and Leaderboard Duration before launching the arena.

Speed Decay Scoring Formula

Points = 1000 * (1 - (timeElapsed / timeLimit) * 0.5)
  • Fast Correct Answers: Correct responses submitted under 1 second scale up to 1000 maximum points.
  • Slower Correct Answers: Scaled linearly down to 500 minimum points as the timer runs down.
  • Incorrect or Unsubmitted: 0 points awarded with instant lock in.
